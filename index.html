How It Works?

Four simple steps to get your item back.

🏷️

1. Add QR Tag

Register your item and generate a unique QR code.

📝

2. Report

If lost or found, submit the item details on the website.

📱

3. Scan

A finder can scan the QR code to view the safe contact page.

🤝

4. Reunite

The owner and finder can arrange a safe return.
